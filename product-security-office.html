EXTERNAL PRODUCT SECURITY OFFICE

Ongoing Product Security. Connected to Your Engineering Team.

Medical device cybersecurity does not end with a submission or product launch. Through an annual agreement, InnoRevision serves as an external product security lead—operating agreed cybersecurity activities, maintaining supporting evidence, and coordinating with engineering, quality, and regulatory teams while the manufacturer retains product and regulatory decision authority.

01

Product Security Baseline & Annual Plan

Review the product portfolio, cybersecurity documentation, responsibilities, and development practices to establish priorities, scope, cadence, and an annual operating plan.

Baseline assessment · Operating plan · Priorities
02

Vulnerability Monitoring & Evaluation

Maintain a process for reviewing relevant vulnerability information and assessing applicability in the context of specific software components, device architecture, and deployment environment.

Vulnerability review · Applicability · Risk decisions
03

SBOM & Third-Party Component Maintenance

Support ongoing SBOM updates, component changes, supplier information review, and traceability between identified dependencies and affected products.

SBOM maintenance · Dependencies · Change review
04

Product Cybersecurity Risk & Evidence

Keep cybersecurity risk documentation, threat assumptions, security controls, and supporting technical records aligned with product changes and emerging information.

Risk records · Threat updates · Technical evidence
05

Release, Update & Incident Coordination

Work alongside engineering, quality, and regulatory teams as software changes, security findings, field issues, or update decisions require product-specific technical review.

Release impact · Remediation coordination · Response
06

Program Reporting & Annual Review

Maintain scheduled status reviews, open-action tracking, management reporting, and an annual assessment of program performance and next-year priorities.

Status reviews · Action tracking · Annual review
