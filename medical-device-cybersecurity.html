MEDICAL DEVICE CYBERSECURITY

Product Security Grounded in How the Device Works.

Effective medical device cybersecurity starts with understanding the product, its interfaces, and its operating environment. InnoRevision connects engineering analysis with security risk management and regulatory evidence aligned with current FDA cybersecurity guidance and, for cyber devices, applicable FD&C Act Section 524B requirements—from early architecture review through premarket submission and postmarket maintenance.

01

Security Architecture & Threat Modeling

Analysis of device components, interfaces, trust boundaries, data flows, attack surfaces, and credible threat scenarios based on the product’s actual design.

Trust boundaries · Attack surfaces · Threat models
02

Security Risk Assessment & Controls

Evaluation of cybersecurity threats, exploitability, potential impacts, and security measures, with attention to interaction between cybersecurity and patient safety risk management.

Risk assessment · Security controls · Residual risk
03

SBOMs & Software Component Analysis

Creation and maintenance of software bills of materials, component inventories, support status, and applicability reviews for third-party and open-source dependencies.

SBOM · SOUP · Component review
04

Security Verification & Testing

Planning and technical review of security testing, static analysis, vulnerability assessment, penetration testing, and evidence that security controls perform as intended.

SAST · Penetration testing · Verification
05

FDA Cybersecurity & Section 524B Support

Development and review of cybersecurity management plans, reports, architecture information, threat models, risk documentation, testing evidence, labeling-related content, SBOMs, and technical responses aligned with current FDA guidance and applicable Section 524B requirements.

FDA cybersecurity · Section 524B · Premarket evidence
06

Postmarket Vulnerability & Update Support

Product-specific vulnerability evaluation, coordinated remediation planning, security update considerations, and maintenance of cybersecurity records over the supported product lifecycle.

Vulnerability evaluation · Updates · Monitoring
